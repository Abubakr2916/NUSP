## Milestone Overview

Milestone 1 focuses on defining the overall scope of NUSP, identifying its main objectives and expected features, and documenting the initial requirements of the proposed system.

NUSP aims to provide a centralized platform where visitors can explore Namal University societies, view their leadership information, browse upcoming events, and access past-event galleries and announcements. Eligible students will be able to apply for society membership or positions in society bodies.

## Key Requirements Identified

* Public access to society information and profiles.
* A unified Upcoming Events section.
* Access to past-event photos and videos.
* Student applications for society bodies according to defined eligibility rules.
* A restriction allowing each student to enroll in only one society.
* No event-registration feature.
* Further clarification of authentication and technical requirements through consultation with university IT.
